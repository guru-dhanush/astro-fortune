## What is a Life Path Number?

Your **Life Path Number** is the most important number in numerology. Derived from your full date of birth, it reveals your core personality, innate strengths, challenges you'll face, and the overarching theme of your life's journey.

Think of it as your cosmic blueprint — the number that stays with you from birth to death, quietly shaping every chapter of your story.

## How to Calculate Your Life Path Number

The process is simple. Reduce your full birth date down to a single digit (or a Master Number: 11, 22, or 33).

**Example: Born on July 14, 1992**

```
Month:  7
Day:    1 + 4 = 5
Year:   1 + 9 + 9 + 2 = 21 → 2 + 1 = 3

Total:  7 + 5 + 3 = 15 → 1 + 5 = 6

Life Path Number = 6
```

> **Note:** If your sum results in 11, 22, or 33 — do not reduce further. These are **Master Numbers** with elevated significance.

## Common Misconceptions

- ❌ *"A bad number means a bad life."* — Every number has light and shadow sides. Awareness is the key to working with yours.
- ❌ *"Only my Life Path matters."* — Numerology is a full system. Your Expression, Soul Urge, and Personal Year numbers add crucial layers.
- ❌ *"Numbers don't change."* — Your core Life Path stays fixed, but **Personal Year numbers** shift annually, creating cycles of growth and rest.
