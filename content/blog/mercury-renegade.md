## What is Mercury Retrograde?

When astronomers say **Mercury is in retrograde**, it means that from our vantage point on Earth, the planet Mercury appears to be moving _backward_ through the sky. This is an optical illusion caused by the relative speeds of Earth and Mercury orbiting the Sun.
In astrology, Mercury rules **communication, technology, travel, and contracts**. When it goes retrograde, these areas of life often experience disruption.

## Mercury Retrograde Dates in 2025

There are **three Mercury retrograde periods** in 2025:

1. **January 18 – February 11** — Capricorn/Aquarius
2. **May 29 – June 22** — Gemini
3. **September 17 – October 9** — Virgo/Libra

## Common Effects

During Mercury retrograde, you may notice:

- Miscommunications and misunderstandings
- Technology glitches and device failures
- Travel delays and mix-ups
- Old friends or ex-partners resurfacing

## What To Avoid

❌ Signing important contracts
❌ Making major purchases (especially electronics)
❌ Starting new projects or businesses
❌ Scheduling important meetings without backup plans

## What To Do Instead

This is actually an excellent time to:

- **Review** — Revisit old projects and finish unfinished business
- **Reflect** — Meditate on past decisions and lessons
- **Revise** — Edit, rewrite, and refine existing work
- **Reconnect** — Reach out to old friends and collaborators
